# Process

The process begins with something we want to do.

We then ask what it would take to do it.

The answer gives us the next step. We take that step, and then ask the same question again:

> **What would it take to do this?**

If the answer is straightforward, we do it.

If the answer requires something else first, that becomes the next thing to do. We follow that through in the same way.

This continues until the original thing is done.

In its simplest form:

```text
want to do something
        ↓
what would it take?
        ↓
take the next step
        ↓
what would it take?
        ↓
take the next step
        ↓
       ...
        ↓
       done
```

The process is recursive because the same process is applied to each step whenever that step requires further work.

There is no requirement to know the entire sequence beforehand. The next step is determined by the situation at hand and by what is necessary to accomplish the current objective.

The details of any particular step depend on the thing being done. The process itself remains the same.

That is the process.
