# Common Sense

I use **common sense** for everything I build.

The process is simple:

**idea → way to do it → do it**

An idea is something I want to make happen in reality. Once I have an idea, I figure out how to do it. This may involve thinking, researching, experimenting, or using whatever other means are necessary to determine a reasonable way forward.

I then do it.

Sometimes the way works. Sometimes it doesn't. When it doesn't, the result tells me something about the way I attempted it, so I figure out another way and try again.

**idea → way to do it → do it → revise → do it → ...**

This continues until the idea is accomplished, changed into something else, or otherwise resolved.

I document the process as I go. The documentation records what I am trying to accomplish, how I intend to accomplish it, what I actually did, and what I learned along the way. This allows each successive step to build on the preceding ones and allows the resulting work to be understood after it has been built.

The same process applies at every level of the work. A large idea can contain smaller ideas, each of which follows the same process. In this way, successive ideas, decisions, attempts, and revisions gradually produce a fully developed result.

There is nothing complicated about the process. I have an idea, I figure out a way to do it, and I do it. Reality provides the result, and that result informs what I do next.
